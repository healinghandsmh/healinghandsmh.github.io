Questions for your insurer

Before scheduling, you may want to ask:

  • Does my plan cover the service I’m seeking?
  • Is the provider in my network?
  • What deductible, copay, or coinsurance applies?
  • Is a referral or prior authorization required?

Please don’t email insurance cards, member IDs, or private health details through regular email.

Common questions
How do I confirm whether my plan is accepted?

Email admin@healinghandsmh.org with a general coverage question. Our team can explain how to confirm your plan and share information appropriately.

What if I have questions about payment?

Reach out before scheduling to discuss payment questions and available options with our team.
