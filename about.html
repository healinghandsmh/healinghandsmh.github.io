Our mission

Our mission is to deliver accessible, compassionate counseling that fosters healing, growth, and resilience for individuals impacted by trauma.

We provide a range of supportive services designed to empower individuals and strengthen our community.

Healing Hands Mental Health serves Chicago and Surrounding Areas, with services in community-based, school-based, probation, and reentry settings.

Whether you are seeking individual support, connection through a group, or a space to work through relationship challenges, we invite you to reach out and learn about the options available.
