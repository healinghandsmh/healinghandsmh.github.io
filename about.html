Who we are

Care begins
with listening.

Healing Hands Mental Health provides Individual Therapy, Group Therapy, and Couples and Marriage Counseling throughout Chicago and Surrounding Areas.

We recognize that each person brings a different story. Our approach centers on understanding your needs, identifying goals together, and supporting meaningful steps forward.

We serve people in community-based, school-based, probation, and reentry settings.
